Our story

INEEDYOU Authentic Wear was founded in 2025 in Mohlakeng, Gauteng, by South African youth entrepreneurs. They first launched a message-driven streetwear brand around one line: "I need you, I'm nothing without you, next by your side."

The label started lean -using pre-orders and print-on-demand intead of buying bulk stock.The first products are white hoodies, oversized tees, sleeveless tops, and tracksuits, all made in South Africa.

Mission

To create authentic,high-quality streetwear that connects people emotionally, while empowering local manufactures and creating jobs for youth.

Visison

To become South Adrica's leading message-driven fashion brand - built from the ground up, by the youth, for the youth.

2. Target Audience

  • Primary:Youth aged 16-35 in townships and urban areas who want affordable statement streetwear.

4.Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths

  • Unique message:"I need you, I'm nothing without you, next by your side" is highly shareable.
  • Lean model: Low risk - no big stock, print only what's needed.
  • Proudly South African: All products are made in South Africa, supporting local manufacturing.
  • Low overhead: Can run from home plus social media.

Weaknesses

  • Brand awareness: It's new and still unknown.
  • Cash flow: limited to R50,000 so growth is slower.
  • Delivery times: Print-on-demand takes 5-7 days.
  • Competition: Competing against big established brands.

How we plan to improve

  • Marketing: organic TikTok/reels plus collabs with 5 micro-influencers, on a R0-R2000 budget.
  • Funding: reinvest first profits and apply for an NYDA grant, to move from R50k to R150k for bulk stock.
  • E-commerce: start with a free linktree and whatsapp business catalogue, then upgrade to a full website.
  • Speed: negotiate a 3-day turnaround with the local printer once order volume increases.
