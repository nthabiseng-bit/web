Welcome to INEEDYOU(Pty)Ltd, trading as I NEED YOU Authentic Wear- a message-driven streetwear brand built by South Africa youth entrepreneurs, made in South Africa.

Where we started

INEEDYOU Authentic Wear was founded in 2025 in Mohlakeng,Gauteng.The label started lean- using pre-orders and print-on-demand instead of buying in bulk stock - with a first drop of white hoodie, oversized tees, sleeveles tops, and tracksuits, all made in South Africa.

You are looking for the full story, mission and vision? Visit the About page.

What are we selling?

Hoddies, tees, vests, and tracksuits, plus custom printing for events. See prices and current promotions on the Products page.
