Our story

INEEDYOU Authentic Wear was founded in 2025 in Mohlakeng, Gauteng, by South African youth entrepreneurs. They launched a message driven strretwear brand around one line: " i need you, i'm nothing without you, next by your side."

The label started lean - using pre-orders and print-on-demand instead of buying bulk stock. The first products are white hoodies, oversized tees, sleeveless tops, and tracksuits, all made in South Africa .

Mission

To create authentic, high-quality streetwear that connects people emotionally, while empowering local manufacturers and creating jobs for youth.

Vision

To become South Africa's leading message-driven fashion brand- built from the ground up, by the youth, for the youth.

2.Target Audience

  • Primary:Youth aged 16-35 in townships and urban areas who want affordable statement strretwear.
  • Secondary:Couples, friends , and creatives who buy into the emotional message.
  • B2B:Small events and organizations that need 20-50 pieces of custom merch.

4.Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths

  • Unique message:"I need you,i'm nothing without you" is highly shareable.
  • Lean model:low risk-no big stock, print only what's needed.
  • Proudly South Africa:local manufacturing supports the community.
  • Low overhead:can run from home plus social media.

Weaknesses

  • Brand awareness:new and still unknown
  • Cash flow:limited to R50,000, so growth is slower
  • Delivery times:Print-on-demand takes 5-7 days
  • Competition:competing against big established brands.

How we plan to improve

  • Marketing:organic Tiktok/reels plus collabs with 5 micro-influencers, on a R0-R2,000 budget
  • Funding:reinvest first profits and apply for an NYDA grant, to move from R50k to R150k for bulk stock.
  • E-commerce:start with a free linktree and whatsapp business catalogue, then upgrade to a full website
  • Speed:negotiate a 3-day turnaround with the local printer once order volume increases.
